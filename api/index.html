AURA is a personal AI assistant with four focused agents — Today for daily planning and tasks, Business for concrete strategy and numbers, Creative for ideas and building things (including live websites), and Memory for what it should remember about you. Every reply comes from a real AI model, grounded in your actual open tasks, the current time, anything you've asked it to remember, and any files or photos you attach. It has genuine live web search for current information, and can build and preview real, downloadable websites on request. Say "hey AURA" any time to wake it, or type or tap the mic. Tasks, memory notes, and voice settings are saved to your account and carry over between visits. AURA is honest about its limits: it cannot touch your phone's real alarms or contacts, publish a website live to the internet, post to your personal social media, or take any action outside this chat — where it can't do something for real, it says so plainly instead of pretending.
